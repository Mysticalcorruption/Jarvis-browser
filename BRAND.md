# JARVIS identity

The built-in image generation tool created `src/ui/jarvis-logo.png`. `src/ui/jarvis.ico` is the Windows icon format of that asset. The source asset is retained in the project.

Generation prompt: “Create a simple sleek app logo for JARVIS personal browser. A single elegant geometric capital J monogram, soft ivory on a solid very dark midnight teal square background #101f26. Rounded, confident stroke with a subtly curved lower hook and a tiny separate circular mint accent above the top right. Minimal Scandinavian technology brand, flat 2D, sharp edges, balanced optical weight, recognizable at 24 pixels. Centered symbol occupies 70 percent of square. No text, no wordmark, no mockup, no additional objects, no glow, no 3D, no gradients. Output one square app icon.”

The UI uses the new mark in the title bar, assistant, favicon and packaged Windows icon. Its palette combines midnight teal, warm ivory and mint, with quieter typography and rounded translucent surfaces.

Design references: [Opera Air](https://www.opera.com/air), [Opera Air's frosted-glass design](https://blogs.opera.com/news/2025/02/opera-air-browser/), and [Vivaldi theme customisation](https://help.vivaldi.com/desktop/appearance-customization/browser-themes/). These informed the visual direction; JARVIS has its own identity and does not claim those browsers' features.
